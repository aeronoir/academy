Questions

Frequently asked

What is the Part 107 exam?

It's the FAA knowledge test required to earn a remote pilot certificate with a small UAS rating, which you need to fly a drone commercially in the U.S. It has 60 multiple-choice questions and a 70% passing score, taken at an FAA-approved testing center. Check the FAA's site for current fees and requirements.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Every question is original, written for study, and based on the FAA's published study guide and regulations. We never copy the live exam, and no one should promise you the exact test.

Is Aero Noir Academy affiliated with the FAA?

No. Aero Noir Academy is an independent training provider. The FAA issues the remote pilot certificate and publishes the official Remote Pilot study guide, which we recommend alongside our materials.

Will this guarantee I pass?

We can't guarantee results, but practicing with explained questions and tracking weak topics gives you the best shot. Study the FAA's materials too.

Do I need a drone to study?

No. The exam is knowledge-based, so you can study without owning an aircraft. A drone helps once you start flying.

Where does my progress save?
