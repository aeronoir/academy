Mission & vision

Mission

Aero Noir Academy exists to put FAA Part 107 certification within reach for career changers who don't see themselves represented in aviation — practical, self-paced training that turns curiosity about drones into a credential and a new professional path.

Vision

A future where the skies reflect the full range of people who dream of flying them. Aero Noir, LLC builds toward that through training, storytelling, and experiences grounded in Black excellence and aviation heritage.
